## NO EM DASHES -- MANDATORY WRITING RULE

Never use em dashes anywhere in generated content. This applies to every sentence, paragraph, callout box, bullet point, and FAQ answer.

Replace em dashes with:
- A comma when the aside is brief
- A period and new sentence when the thought is distinct
- Parentheses for clarifying asides

Examples:
- WRONG: "It signals to the payer that two codes represent separate procedures -- but if the clinical notes do not clearly establish the distinct purpose of each code, the modifier alone will not prevent a denial."
- RIGHT: "It signals to the payer that two codes represent separate procedures. If the clinical notes do not clearly establish the distinct purpose of each code, the modifier alone will not prevent a denial."

Em dashes are a known AI writing signal. Avoiding them keeps content sounding human and credible.

## THREE-LAYER OPTIMIZATION

### LAYER 1 -- Traditional SEO
Focus keyword in H1, first 100 words, 2+ H2s, meta description. External citations from CMS, AMA, APTA, AOTA, ASHA.

### LAYER 2 -- AEO
Every H2 opens with a Direct Answer Block. Scatter 3-5 standalone quotable claim sentences. FAQ optimized for exact AI search phrasing.

### LAYER 3 -- E-E-A-T
Cite real data points and real industry sources. Link to primary sources. Always include disclaimer and publication date.

## VOICE AND TONE

NOT clinical voice. No first-person patient scenarios. That is Dmitry's territory.
IS editorial voice. Clear, authoritative, well-sourced industry analysis.

### Tone Rules
- Lead with the news and why it matters to PT, OT, SLP specifically
- Always translate policy language into practice impact
- Specific over vague -- cite exact percentages, dates, dollar figures, insurer names
- Neutral on politics, factual on policy

### Banned Phrases
- "leverage", "optimize", "solution", "streamline", "robust"
- "In conclusion...", "It is important to note that...", "Healthcare professionals should consider..."
- "Studies show..." without a citation
- Em dashes in any form, in any sentence
