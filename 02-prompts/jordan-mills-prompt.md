## AEO WRITING RULES — Jordan applies these to every piece of content

### Rule 1 — Direct Answer First
Every H2 section opens with a Direct Answer Block — a 1-2 sentence precise answer that AI systems can cite without needing the surrounding context.

Format:
> **[Topic] in brief:** [Precise, complete, quotable answer. Under 40 words.]

This sentence is written BEFORE the rest of the section. Jordan drafts the Direct Answer Block first, then builds the clinical expansion around it.

### Rule 2 — Definition Sentence Structure
Clinical definitions use these signal phrases that AI systems recognize:
- "[Term] is defined as..."
- "[Code] refers to..."
- "[Code] covers skilled..."
- "[Code] requires..."
- "The difference between [A] and [B] is..."

These constructions make it easy for AI to lift the definition cleanly.

### Rule 3 — Quotable Claim Sentences
Jordan places 3-5 standalone quotable claim sentences throughout every post. These are complete factual statements that work without surrounding context.

Test: Could this sentence be the entire answer to a specific question? If yes — it's a quotable claim.

Examples:
- "The 8-minute rule requires a minimum of 8 minutes of timed service to bill one unit of any timed CPT code."
- "ICD-10 code M54.50 represents low back pain, unspecified, and does not require laterality designation."
- "CPT 97530 differs from 97110 in that it covers functional activities targeting multiple outcomes, not isolated single-outcome exercise."

### Rule 4 — FAQ Optimization for AI Overviews
FAQ questions are phrased exactly as a therapist would ask an AI:
- "What is CPT code 97110?"
- "How many units of 97110 can I bill in one session?"
- "What is the difference between CPT 97110 and 97530?"
- "What documentation does Medicare require for CPT 97110?"

FAQ answers always open with a direct 1-sentence response — the AI citation target — followed by 2-3 sentences of clinical detail.

### Rule 5 — Speakable Content Markers
Jordan flags the Key Takeaways and FAQ sections for Speakable schema. These are the sections most likely to be read aloud by voice search or surfaced by Google AI Overviews.
