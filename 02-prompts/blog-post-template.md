## NO EM DASHES -- MANDATORY WRITING RULE

Never use em dashes anywhere in generated content. This applies to every section, every sentence, every example, every callout box, and every FAQ answer throughout the entire blog post.

Replace em dashes with:
- A comma when the aside is brief
- A period and new sentence when the thought is distinct
- Parentheses for clarifying asides

Examples:
- WRONG: "CPT 97110 is a timed code -- billed in 15-minute units -- for therapeutic exercise."
- RIGHT: "CPT 97110 is a timed code billed in 15-minute units for therapeutic exercise."
- WRONG: "Medical necessity is not established by describing the intervention -- it is established by connecting the intervention to a documented functional outcome."
- RIGHT: "Medical necessity is not established by describing the intervention. It is established by connecting the intervention to a documented functional outcome."

Em dashes are a known AI writing signal. Avoiding them keeps content sounding human and credible.

## AEO WRITING RULES -- Apply to Every Section

### AEO Rule 1 -- Direct Answer First
Every H2 section must open with a Direct Answer Block before any expansion.
Format:
> **[Topic] in one sentence:** [Clean, quotable, 15-25 word answer that AI systems can cite directly.]

Example for CPT 97110:
> **CPT 97110 in one sentence:** CPT 97110 is a timed therapeutic exercise code billed in 15-minute units for skilled interventions targeting strength, endurance, range of motion, or flexibility.

### AEO Rule 2 -- Definition-First H2 Structure
1. Direct Answer Block (1-2 sentences)
2. Clinical expansion (Dr. D voice)
3. Specific example or scenario
4. Supporting bullet list or table if relevant

### AEO Rule 3 -- Quotable Claim Sentences
Scatter 3-5 quotable claim sentences throughout every post:
- "The 8-minute rule requires a minimum of 8 minutes of timed service to bill one unit of CPT 97110."
- "ICD-10 code M54.50 represents low back pain, unspecified, and is one of the most frequently used codes in outpatient physical therapy."
- "CPT 97530 differs from 97110 in that it covers functional activities with multiple outcome targets, not isolated single-muscle interventions."

### AEO Rule 4 -- Structured Answer Formatting
- Bold the key term at the start of definition sentences
- Use "is defined as", "refers to", "means", "requires" phrasing
- Keep definition sentences under 30 words
- Never bury the answer -- always lead with it

### AEO Rule 5 -- FAQ Optimization for AI Overviews
Every FAQ question must:
- Mirror the exact phrasing a therapist would type or speak into an AI
- Open its answer with a direct 1-sentence response
- Follow with 2-3 sentences of clinical detail
- Never start the answer with "Great question" or any filler
