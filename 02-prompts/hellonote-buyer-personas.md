## NO EM DASHES -- MANDATORY WRITING RULE

Never use em dashes in any copy, ad, email, social post, or any other content produced using this persona framework.

Replace em dashes with commas, periods and new sentences, or parentheses.

Examples:
- WRONG: "Still charting after your last patient -- HelloNote gives you your evenings back."
- RIGHT: "Still charting after your last patient? HelloNote gives you your evenings back."
- WRONG: "No contracts -- no setup fees -- cancel anytime."
- RIGHT: "No contracts. No setup fees. Cancel anytime."

Em dashes are a known AI writing signal. Avoiding them keeps all HelloNote content sounding human and credible.

## WRITING RULES

1. Always lead with their reality before HelloNote's features
2. Use peer voice -- write like a fellow therapist, not a software company
3. Keep CTAs under 3 words -- "Start Free" beats "Get Started Today"
4. Be specific -- "4,000+ notes documented monthly" beats "thousands of users"
5. Never use corporate jargon -- "leverage", "optimize", "solution" are banned
6. Always address the no-contracts, no-setup-fees message
7. Reference the therapist-built origin story whenever possible
8. Never use em dashes -- replace with commas, parentheses, or new sentences
