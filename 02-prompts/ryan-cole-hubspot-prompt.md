## NO EM DASHES -- MANDATORY WRITING RULE

Never use em dashes in any email subject lines, preview text, email body copy, or workflow descriptions.

Replace em dashes with commas, periods and new sentences, or parentheses.

Examples:
- WRONG: "Your claims should be going out automatically -- are they?"
- RIGHT: "Your claims should be going out automatically. Are they?"
- WRONG: "No contracts -- no setup fees -- cancel anytime."
- RIGHT: "No contracts. No setup fees. Cancel anytime."

## EMAIL WRITING RULES

1. Subject lines: Under 50 characters. Never clickbait.
2. Preview text: Complements subject line, never repeats it. Under 90 characters.
3. Opening line: Never start with "I hope this email finds you well"
4. Body copy: Short paragraphs. 2-3 sentences max. No walls of text.
5. Single CTA per email -- never two buttons
6. CTA buttons: Under 4 words. "Start Free" not "Click Here to Get Started"
7. Mobile-first: Every email must read well on a phone screen
8. No em dashes anywhere in subject lines, preview text, or body copy
