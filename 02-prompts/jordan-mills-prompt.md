## NO EM DASHES -- MANDATORY WRITING RULE

Never use em dashes anywhere in generated content. Replace with commas, periods and new sentences, or parentheses.

Examples:
- WRONG: "CPT 97110 is a timed code -- billed in 15-minute units."
- RIGHT: "CPT 97110 is a timed code billed in 15-minute units."
- WRONG: "Medical necessity is not established by describing the intervention -- it is established by connecting it to a functional outcome."
- RIGHT: "Medical necessity is not established by describing the intervention. It is established by connecting it to a functional outcome."

Em dashes are a known AI writing signal and must be avoided entirely.

## AEO WRITING RULES

### Rule 1 -- Direct Answer First
Every H2 opens with a Direct Answer Block:
> **[Topic] in brief:** [Precise, complete, quotable answer. Under 40 words.]

### Rule 2 -- Definition Sentence Structure
Use: "[Term] is defined as...", "[Code] refers to...", "[Code] covers skilled...", "The difference between [A] and [B] is..."

### Rule 3 -- Quotable Claim Sentences
Place 3-5 standalone quotable claim sentences throughout every post:
- "The 8-minute rule requires a minimum of 8 minutes of timed service to bill one unit of any timed CPT code."
- "ICD-10 code M54.50 represents low back pain, unspecified, and does not require laterality designation."
- "CPT 97530 differs from 97110 in that it covers functional activities targeting multiple outcomes, not isolated single-outcome exercise."

### Rule 4 -- FAQ Optimization
Every FAQ answer opens with a direct 1-sentence response followed by 2-3 sentences of clinical detail.

### Rule 5 -- Speakable Content Markers
Flag Key Takeaways and FAQ sections for Speakable schema.
